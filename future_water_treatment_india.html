Technology trends

ZLD

High-priority in industrial reuse and discharge-sensitive sectors; strongest where compliance risk is highest.

RO

Still the core workhorse technology, but cost, membrane fouling and reject management remain constraints.

AI & IoT

Used for predictive maintenance, remote monitoring, leakage detection and asset optimization.

Decentralized

Useful in urban clusters, institutions and Tier-2/3 geographies where centralized infrastructure lags.

Regulation drives demand

Public policy

  • Jal Jeevan Mission expands potable water infrastructure
  • National Water Mission pushes conservation and 20% efficiency gains
  • Urban and rural water quality monitoring increases treatment need

Compliance pressure

  • CPCB and SPCB norms sustain wastewater treatment demand
  • Reuse and ZLD expectations intensify in selected sectors and locations
  • BRSR water and GHG metrics make measurement commercially relevant

Where to play

Highest-potential opportunities

  • Industrial wastewater recycling
  • Tier-2/3 urban treatment infrastructure
  • Smart water management overlays
  • PPP and long-term O&M contracts

Key risks

  • High capex and execution complexity
  • Regulatory and approval delays
  • Technology cost and energy burden
  • Fragmented market with price-led bidding
